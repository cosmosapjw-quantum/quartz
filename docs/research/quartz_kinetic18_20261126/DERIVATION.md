# Joint utility gaps and decision reliability
Let U_i ∈ {0,1/2,1}, i=0,1,2; anchor i=0. D=(D1,D2)=(U1-U0,U2-U0), D0=0. The feasible lattice S consists of (k/2,l/2), k,l integers with max(0,k,l)-min(0,k,l)<=2. Necessity from U spread; sufficiency choose 2U0=-min(0,k,l). Exactly 19 states. Conditional law p(s|F) over coarsened observable features F in a specified root population. Individual deterministic game labels are not random repeated draws. Define m_i=E_p[D_i], H=E_p[max_i D_i], V=max_i m_i, Bayes panel risk R=H-V. Stop at anchor incurs H; optimal correction achieves expected improvement V>=0. Marginal means suffice for terminal selection; joint dependence is essential for H and subsequent information value. E.g. D1,D2 both Bernoulli on {0,.5} with identical marginals, perfectly correlated gives H=.25, anticorrelated gives H=.5; both V=.25. Therefore equal marginal forecast accuracy does not identify joint regret or VOC.
For approximating q>0 on S, choose d_q=argmax_i E_q[D_i] (ties anchor then panel order); d_p similarly. Actual excess conditional risk E_p[D_dp-D_dq]≥0. g(s)=D_dp-D_dq=U_dp-U_dq ∈[-1,1]. E_q g<=0. Then E_p g<=sum_s |p-q|=2TV(p,q)<=sqrt(2 KL(p||q)) by Pinsker, capped at 1. If true conditional TV <=epsilon and q recommends switching with E_q D_i>2epsilon, true expected gain positive. This is not an empirically verified TV radius; no per-position safety from calibration averages. For known exact proof intervals l_i<=U_i<=u_i, a deterministic stop loss bound for decision d is max(0,max_{i≠d}u_i-l_d), requiring intervals on all relevant legal actions. For candidate-panel risk, adding an oracle omission term restores full legal loss. No certified STOP from q alone.
Joint losses L_log=-log q(s), L_Brier=sum_t(q(t)-1[t=s])². Expected excess vs p equals KL(p||q) and ||q-p||² respectively. Better mean log loss across roots does not imply pointwise regret reduction or even mean decision improvement relative to a different model. Counterexample binary utility gap ±1 with p(+)=.51, q1(+)=.49 and q2(+)=.90; q1 has lower KL but wrong decision; q2 optimal. Conditional error bound is relative to true p, not estimated from differences of empirical test scores. For nested F⊂G with coherent p, K17 access and information decompositions remain exact; fitted p(F),p(G) independently need not satisfy tower; no VOC estimate from a static predictor.
