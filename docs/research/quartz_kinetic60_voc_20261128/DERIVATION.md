Take partition b=(h, I(2*d>=20-h), I(a_h=prior_best)); d=distance to raw leader flip, eligible iff no full lock and nondominated rival exists. For each cell, average per-family means then across represented families; this estimates equal-family conditional cell means, not a Bayesian posterior or globally occupancy-weighted estimate. Conservative unseen/underrepresented fallback continues. Gate STOP if supported and mean G-lambda*K<=0. Compare to full Q20 at each checkpoint; a later continuation may stop earlier than full Q20. This is one-step lookahead heuristic, not Bellman optimal or a policy-improvement theorem. For actual finite-horizon policy improvement with exact V_rho and all states pointwise dominance, telescoping implies nonnegative utility difference; bin averaging and changed occupancy violate these conditions.
Counterexample: two equally frequent states in a cell, G=.5 and -.5, K=1 in each; average net negative at any positive lambda but one state incurs real harm. Selection can shift occupancy toward harmful state; aggregate performance need not improve. Hence frozen fresh full-game test, not root accuracy alone, is decisive.
