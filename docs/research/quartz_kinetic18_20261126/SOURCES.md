# Primary sources and transfer
- Gneiting & Raftery (2007), Strictly Proper Scoring Rules, Prediction, and Estimation, JASA 102:359–378, §2.2, §3.1 examples 1 and 3, https://sites.stat.washington.edu/raftery/Research/PDF/Gneiting2007jasa.pdf . Quadratic and log scores elicit categorical distributions; expected excess loss equals squared L2 and KL respectively. These identities refer to forecast distributions and actual outcome law, not i.i.d. tree returns. Use positive losses rather than rewards.
- Frazier et al., Correlated Knowledge Gradient §3 Eq.(11), https://people.orie.cornell.edu/pfrazier/pub/Correlated_main_paper.pdf (reused K17 source): max posterior mean improvement. Joint outcome law useful for risk and later information transition; does not itself determine VOC without a conditional observation kernel.
- Sahoo et al., Reliable Decisions with Threshold Calibration, NeurIPS 2021, §2.3, https://proceedings.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2021/file/0e65972dce68dad4d52d063967f0a705-Paper.pdf : average calibration does not guarantee threshold-decision loss calibration; threshold losses are not our general joint action setting.
- Marx, Zalouk, Ermon (2023), Calibration by Distribution Matching, NeurIPS, §6.3, https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/file/52493d82db00e73abb2858a5a5f28717-Paper-Conference.pdf : decision-dependent calibration objectives and kernel MMD. Our kernel histogram is a different conditional density estimator, not their calibration algorithm.
- SciSpace Zhao et al. 2021 decision calibration abstract, https://scispace.com/papers/calibrating-predictions-to-decisions-a-novel-approach-to-4aunxl9gqd : distribution indistinguishability for downstream decisions, not established here.
- Original Quartz thesis P1 and K17 REPORT/DERIVATION, actual native observations. No novelty priority claim for proper scores or TV bounds.
