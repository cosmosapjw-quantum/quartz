# Literature and authority
Hay et al., Selecting Computations: Theory and Applications, https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.5879, Definitions 1 and 3: utility of selected arm less computation cost, stop as metalevel action. Sezener and Dayan, Static and Dynamic Values of Computation in MCTS, https://scispace.com/papers/static-and-dynamic-values-of-computation-in-mcts-2vpb9zrroh (abstract: value from eventual chosen action, greedy optimal under conditions). Callaway et al., Learning to select computations, https://proceedings.mlr.press/r16/callaway18a.html (approximate metalevel policy search, myopic vs perfect information). These justify a costed one-step approximation, not a theorem for our finite NN surrogate. K47 exact root proof and deterministic native PUCT are authority. K47 measured risk sign but magnitude was very small. No claim of novelty or posterior optimality.
