# R03 — definitions, assumptions and claim alignment

## State and evidence boundary

X is the complete public cue vector for one legal TicTacToe root. U_a in {0,1/2,1} is a root-player normalized exact utility of child a. It is dimensionless game reward, not physical energy. Each query reveals U_a exactly once. The controller's history h contains every queried value and all public terminal values. The initial compulsory exact query creates a deployable incumbent; it is charged in measured full runtime. An already terminal winning candidate needs no paid query.

Only candidates with exact known values may be deployed. Thus STOP reward is b_h=max known U, not max over posterior means of unqueried candidates. S_h is the set of unqueried nonterminal children. H is the remaining count of additional queries: starts at2 and decrements. Replanning H=2 after every query while allowing more than2 is outside this contract.

A proposed joint law is a statistical approximation conditional on X. Deterministic minimax values across real roots are not literally generated by the latent variable. Generalization of that approximation is an empirical obligation.

## Two controls

PRODUCT_H2 uses the product of common cue-conditioned marginal laws before the initial query and keeps cross-candidate beliefs fixed. JOINT_H2 conditions on the initial reveal and every later reveal. This primary contrast estimates the combined initial-and-later propagation mechanism.

INITIAL_PROJECTED_H2 starts from the product of JOINT_H2's conditional marginals after the same initial reveal h0, then freezes them. The secondary contrast isolates later propagation. These are different estimands; neither difference is silently attributed solely to later queries.

Initialization, legal actions, terminal treatment, cost estimates, tie ordering and maximum query count must be identical. A shared initial exact observation is included once, never twice.

## Candidate family

For base p_a, define mu_a=sum_u u p_a(u), s_a=max over positive support |u-mu_a|, f_a(u)=(u-mu_a)/s_a. If s_a=0 set f_a=0. Proposed components are p_a^z(u)=p_a(u)[1+z rho f_a(u)], z in {-1,+1}, with equal initial weights, 0<=rho<1. Their mixture is P_rho(U)=1/2 product p_a^+ +1/2 product p_a^-.

This is a one-parameter positive common-factor family. It preserves every pre-reveal marginal exactly, but cannot express arbitrary dependence or negative pairwise covariance. rho and -rho differ only by latent labels; use rho>=0. A terminal degenerate candidate has f=0.

## Costs and approximation

d_a is a predicted future exact-query charge in utility units. Empirical net reward must also charge the actual cue, initialization, posterior and planner runtime. Finite Bellman optimality applies only to the stated model and included costs. Unmodeled planner overhead and wrong predictive costs are explicit approximation errors, even if the implementation is arithmetically exact.

Node price lambda_N has units utility/node; wall price lambda_t has units utility/second. A TRAIN-only conversion rule will be frozen in R09 before evaluating new CAL/TEST. No physical c, hbar or k_B has been set to1; they are absent from this dimensionless decision model.
