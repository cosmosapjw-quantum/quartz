## Verdict

The motivation remains worthwhile: allocate more computation where a decision can improve, maintain alternative candidates, stop when further work has low expected benefit, and couple this with actual hardware costs. The source does not establish beyond-AlphaZero performance, calibrated Bayesian search beliefs, a composed value-of-computation controller, or even the frozen ≥30% NN-call saving target with non-inferior play. Latest source contains a real A01 STOP mechanism and broad experimental plumbing, while most other Foundry axes remain proxies, skeletons, or readouts.

Specification verdict: **partial/inconclusive** for the research propositions. Production engineering verdict: not assessed for release. Fresh source inspection establishes concrete mismatches below; historical result values are reported by repository documents and were not independently recomputed because raw artifacts are absent.

## Ten concrete blockers / invalid claim mechanisms

1. **The live A01 council does not receive the implemented H1 signal.** `src/mcts/foundry/policy.rs:117-127` computes a top-two Gaussian tail; `:191-192` supplies `h1_stability = 1 - p_flip` and `p_flip` from the same value. `src/mcts/foundry/control.rs:36-43` then takes `max(1-H1, P_flip, omission)`. On this bridge the first two terms are exactly duplicates. Calling this an independent H1/P_flip ensemble, transferring the offline H1 calibration result, or interpreting agreement between the two as corroboration would be false. Wire the real Dirichlet H1 estimator or relabel the signal; then calibrate the joint stop risk on held-out position families.

2. **Candidate omission protection is fed a false visibility count.** `src/mcts/mod.rs:397-421` sets `n_visible = n_children` at `:413`, while `:425-489` constructs EdgeViews only from materialized `read_edges()`. `src/mcts/foundry/policy.rs:194-198` therefore supplies omission_bound=0. `src/mcts/foundry/search.rs:93-110,123-128` similarly cannot activate outside-candidate widening when told all candidates are visible. Source does not establish full-support candidate safety. Populate the true candidate/materialized counts and an explicit tail observation channel; certificates must retain unobserved candidates with conservative upper bounds.

3. **`kg_per_arm` is not one-computation KG under the cited Gaussian definition.** `src/mcts/policy/kg_stop.rs:86-120` computes EI using current gap standard deviation `sqrt(sigma2_a/(n_a+lambda0) + sigma2_b/(n_b+lambda0))`. It has no update model specifying how a proposed computation shifts the posterior mean. `:130-153` forces the leader's KG to zero. The mathematical expectation formula itself can be correct while its scale is wrong for `E[max μ_after] - max μ_now`. This remains unchanged from main except formatting/default telemetry. Replace the scale with the predictive distribution of the posterior-mean update, including leader measurements and cross-arm covariance, or explicitly label the existing value as a residual uncertainty/EI proxy.

4. **Latest A04 allocation is an additional uncalibrated proxy, not that KG implementation.** `src/mcts/foundry/control.rs:231-235` uses `(sigma+best_sigma) * exp(-gap/(sigma+best_sigma))`; `:263-266` sets an asserted LCB to `0.5*gain` with confidence 0.25. The constant factor is not a confidence bound. Its cost defaults to 1 ms/eval (`:222-227`). An axis status, `activation_guard` string, and numeric `regret_reduction_lcb` field do not provide empirical or mathematical calibration. Keep shadow-only until forked interventions fit and validate expected gain and uncertainty.

5. **The live belief is neither correlation-aware nor calibrated uncertainty of action value.** `src/mcts/policy/trait_def.rs:104-135` shrinks per-backup sample variance toward dispersion of root action means. This is not automatically posterior uncertainty of the stationary target action value. Live A01 uses this raw per-backup sigma at `foundry/policy.rs:124-127` without dividing by an effective sample count, without low-count eligibility, without pair covariance, and without drift/bias channels. `src/mcts/mod.rs:418` supplies no evaluator uncertainty. `docs/QUARTZ_THEORY.md:152-158` additionally treats dispersion *between actions* as evaluator uncertainty/confidence: separated known actions can have high dispersion, equally biased uncertain actions low dispersion. Large N can shrink Monte Carlo noise while leaving NN bias, shared-leaf dependence, and adaptive-depth drift intact. Existing `UncertaintyChannels` schema does not estimate these channels.

6. **Parallel sufficient statistics and stale-stop handling are not a certificate foundation.** `src/mcts/backup.rs:53-74` separately updates N, reads W, then updates W and M2; its own module comments `:9-32` acknowledge this is not an atomic Welford recurrence under concurrent backup. A stable f64 representation does not remove that statistical mismatch. KG's `KgCache.n_total_at_observe` is diagnostic; `kg_stop.rs:372-390` does not compare it with current visits or candidate identity. KL-LUCB also uses cached confidence state. The standalone `PolicyCache::is_stale_for` (`policy/cache.rs:110-121`) exists but is not used by those old wrappers. Latest A01 improves freshness with root-local policy identity and same-visit/iteration halt (`foundry/policy.rs:260-265`), but it still inherits the snapshot/statistical issues. Use coherent boundary snapshots or mergeable completed-block moments; explicitly invalidate confidence after candidate or model changes.

7. **Pending work is exposed but not part of a live marginal information/cost scheduler.** `EdgeView.n_virtual` exists and `foundry/search.rs:388-405` sums it, but A13 emits Noop. A15 proposes fixed targets at `:458-505`; the live A01 bridge fills `RuntimeSnapshot::default()` at `foundry/policy.rs:200`, so queue wait, latency, pending and overlap are dummy values. Old KG prices elapsed wall time divided by launched iterations (`kg_stop.rs:289-298`); `SearchSnapshot` explicitly says iteration differs from completed root visits in parallel (`trait_def.rs:53-56`). This average is not the marginal time of another batch, and already-pending work should not be counted again as independent new information. Record service events and pending leaf/root/epoch identities and assess conditional incremental value.

8. **The controller has no composed live action menu.** `foundry/policy.rs:55-62` permits only A01 config; `:225-229` instantiates only A01; `:82-86` executes STOP/Noop; `:256-257` has identity score adjustment. A06 enumerates first edges (`foundry/search.rs:42-68`) rather than executing a Gumbel bracket, A08 emits no proposal (`:179-181`), and A11 only emits ResampleMode proposals (`:307-338`). The existing interfaces are useful integration hooks, not evidence that widening, pending scheduling, rejuvenation, or tactical alternatives actually influence search. Do not activate all axes at once; build a minimal, measured SAMPLE/REOPEN/STOP loop first.

9. **Readout agreement and reference KL are not objective play quality.** Main `docs/PHASE15_REEVALUATION.md:204-244` identifies same-checkpoint high-budget A0 as a reference rather than ground truth and `:248-276` distinguishes posthoc readout from online control. Its opening status notes several infrastructure fixes; do not repeat every historical defect as current. Latest B13 evidence remains KL-only and decision-neutral (`docs/CLAIM_LEDGER.md:77`). B3 gains additionally spend extra budget (latest ledger `:59`). Reusing budget prefixes is excellent pairing, but creates correlated rows. A0/B5 aliases must not become extra competitors/replications. Use exact tactical outcomes when available, diverse stronger references otherwise, and paired games for quality claims.

10. **Historical counts/green gates cannot supply missing scientific independence or provenance.** Latest `docs/CLAIM_LEDGER.md:114-118` explicitly limits 26-axis contract completion and analysis to infrastructure. `:120-143` quarantines the August campaigns: hybrid/unclosed lineage, failed generalization NI, resource-frontier changes, dependent/invalidated estimands, and dormant A10. The 960-game aggregate is descriptive, not 960 independent research replications. Passing 140 numerical tests (BQ++ design `:121-139`), 26 schema gates, 6912 repeated readout rows, or a source-hash check does not prove efficacy. Preserve failure rows and analyze the correct clusters: training seed family, opening/game family, continuation tree, and reused budget prefix.
