# R06: independent mathematical checks of C29 derivation

Two distinct finite proofs were audited: (A) exact factorized filtering under a shared fixed U_i and conditional independence given Z; (B) unnormalized Bellman recursion with nonzero costs and supported observation branches. They are not proofs of calibrated game-model quality.

(A) Directly apply Bayes to P(z,u|h)=q_z prod_j pi_{j,z}(u_j). After multiplying by C_i(y|u_i), summing all U_j,j!=i gives D(y)=sum_z q_z a_iz(y). The resulting q'_z and pi'_{i,z} have exactly the R03 formulas; other candidate laws are unchanged conditional on Z. Repeat uses the unchanged fixed U_i, so the same local law is multiplied by another likelihood; it is *incorrect* to reset pi_i to its initial prior after each observation. If D=0, conditional distribution is undefined, not a NaN state. If a_iz=0, q'_z=0 and the component's arbitrary conditional local state is unobservable until zero likelihood remains zero. The induction starts at h empty and preserves normalization.

(B) G_h(W)=Z(W)V_h(W). At h=0 the identity follows from linearity of expected terminal utility and maximization over selectable actions. For h>0, multiply the normalized sum_y Z_y/Z V_(h-1)(W_y) by Z: obtain sum_y G_(h-1)(W_y). Since cost is -c_i Z, it is charged exactly once before each query, independent of outcome. This holds at Z=0 by G(0)=0, preserving zero branch validity, and any nonnegative scaling of W scales every term. It does not assume every action's observation Y is independent of U or other Y; only the explicit channel likelihood and complete-state representation are needed for this world recursion. Factor compression additionally assumes conditional independence.

Counterexample attacks: (i) scalar m unchanged while local beliefs and terminal decisions change, established by independent Bernoulli plus BSC 3/4; (ii) replacing posterior-mean terminal by C28 known-only mode changes the problem, so no false cross-contract equality; (iii) requerying a fixed U differs from independently resampling U and gives repeated-evidence updating, not an independent-arm bandit process; (iv) a zero observation branch is omitted, never normalized by a zero mass; (v) negative covariance sign does not imply independent variance is always conservative.

Orthogonal Wolfram Language exact-rational evaluation actually returned:
Out[1] = {133/200, 543/800, 11/800, 9/10}
for one- and two-query Bernoulli fixture values, nonmyopic increment and posterior after two positive signals. This is a separate CAS evaluation of the arithmetic, not a native implementation review or generalized mathematical theorem.

Review verdict: R03–R05 exact conditional derivations hold under the recorded assumptions. Remaining issue for R07 is to execute two independent computational representations (complete-world unnormalized and factorized normalized) for fixed and randomized finite inputs, support/endpoint controls and C28 known-only perfect-reveal compatibility. No strong no-go or new POMDP theorem is proposed.
