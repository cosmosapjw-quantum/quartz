# Simultaneous trace-cost envelope and resource-constrained Gibbs plans
Let X be the full observed prefix pool and C its complete 8 x 3 nonnegative potential TRACE incremental costs under a fixed protocol. Fit strictly positive d_ij(X) on disjoint FIT. For m exchangeable calibration pools and one new exchangeable full TRACE pool (conditional on frozen FIT), use scalar S_p=max_ij C_pij/d_pij. For 0<alpha<1 let k=ceil((m+1)(1-alpha)). If k<=m, a=max(1,S_(k)); otherwise a=infinity (no finite distribution-free bound). By exchangeability and randomized tie ranks, the new score rank is uniform on 1..m+1; on rank<=k the new score is <=S_(k) (inclusive ties conservative). Hence P(all C_ij<=a d_ij)>=k/(m+1)>=1-alpha when k<=m. No independence among roots or options needed. This is marginal over new pools/calibration, not conditional coverage at an arbitrary F. A subset of coordinates selected by any measurable plan has simultaneous coverage on that same event; if live execution costs differ from counterfactual TRACE, or timing distributions drift, the implication does not follow. Need fresh CHECK and physical live overrun observations. Overhead and planner/scheduling/IPC cost not bounded by TRACE envelope. Quantize q_ij=ceil(a d_ij/Delta), q_i0=0, K=floor(B/Delta). On joint event and planned sum q<=K, selected TRACE costs sum<=B; no hard deterministic deadline claim. Coordinate-wise 90% bounds alone can leave joint coverage as low as 80% for two disjoint 10% failure events; for 24 coordinates the union bound is vacuous. A common max-score is conservative; compare point and robust DP.
Original Eq1 Gibbs generalization: finite feasible plan space A_B, prior Pi(j)=product_i pi_ij positive, normalized after restricting to A_B. Maximize E_Q G - beta^{-1} KL(Q||Pi_B), G=sum_i g_ij. Identity via relative entropy yields Q_beta(j)=Pi_B(j) exp(beta G(j))/Z, free energy beta^{-1} log Z. If priors uniform across feasible plans, MAP maximizes G for any beta>0; if nonuniform, finite-beta MAP maximizes G+beta^{-1} log Pi_B; beta->infty concentrates on max-G plans with prior-relative ties. Cost feasibility is independent of temperature. Unnormalized partition recursion Z_i(b)=sum_{j:q_ij<=b} pi_ij exp(beta g_ij) Z_{i-1}(b-q_ij), Z_0(b)=1 for b>=0; normalizing by Z at beta=0 gives restricted-prior partition. Exact O(n K J) recursion, log-sum-exp for numerical implementation. This is a mathematically explicit return to the original finite-temperature prior over actions, now over feasible compute plans. Current coding uses point and robust deterministic DP/ratio, not sampled Gibbs. No assertion of physical thermodynamics.
