# R03: exact posterior sufficient-state derivation and scalar counterexample

Assume a binary Z in {-1,+1}, q_z=P(Z=z|h), and fixed candidate utilities U_i independent conditional on Z. Their initial component laws are p_i(u)(1+z r_i f_i(u)); each is normalized because E_p f_i=0. Conditional on U_i, every new query to candidate i draws a fresh Y from C_i(y|U_i), independent of earlier draws. The same fixed U_i is not redrawn.

After a history h_i of noisy signals from i, define L_{i,z}(h_i)=sum_u p_i(u)(1+z r_i f_i(u)) prod_{y in h_i} C_i(y|u). The complete posterior factorizes:
P(Z=z,U_1=u_1,...,U_n=u_n|h)=q_z(h) prod_i pi_{i,z}(u_i|h_i),
q_z(h) proportional to q_z^0 prod_i L_{i,z}(h_i), and
pi_{i,z}(u|h_i)=p_i(u)(1+z r_i f_i(u))prod C_i(y|u) / L_{i,z}(h_i) when L>0.
This is a sufficient statistic (q_z, pi_{i,z}) for all future query observations and STOP decisions under the fixed likelihood and costs. The full joint belief is sufficient more generally; the factor state is exact only under conditional independence.

For a fresh observation Y_i=y, let a_{i,z}(y)=sum_u pi_{i,z}(u) C_i(y|u), and D(y)=sum_z q_z a_{i,z}(y). On supported D>0:
q'_z=q_z a_{i,z}(y)/D;
pi'_{i,z}(u)=pi_{i,z}(u) C_i(y|u)/a_{i,z}(y), if a_{i,z}(y)>0;
pi'_{j,z}=pi_{j,z} for j != i.
When a_{i,z}=0 the updated weight q'_z is zero; the zero-weight local law can be left unchanged without changing any observable. If D=0 the observation is impossible and is excluded; never normalize by zero.

Exact counterexample to scalar m sufficiency. Take r_i=0 for all i, independent Bernoulli U_1,U_2 with probabilities 1/2, BSC observation accuracy 3/4 and incumbent b=0. The latent q_z and m=0 are unchanged for all histories. Before querying, STOP value T=max(1/2,1/2)=1/2. After observing Y_1=1 once, pi(U_1=1)=3/4 and T=3/4, although m=0 remains exactly. The predictive P(Y_1=1) changes from 1/2 before observation to (3/4)(3/4)+(1/4)(1/4)=5/8 afterwards. Equal m, different STOP values AND future observation kernels, hence no scalar-m policy can be generally sufficient. This is a constructive mathematical counterexample, not a claim that no lower-dimensional sufficient statistic exists on every restricted history family.

The n-signal state size is O(n * 2 * |U|) rather than |U|^n for the exact factor update. This storage bound is conditional on known fixed channels, conditional independence, one binary latent and fixed utility law. It is not a bound on total Bellman history enumeration.
