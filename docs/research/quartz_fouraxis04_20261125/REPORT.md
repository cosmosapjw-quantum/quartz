## Theory and correction
Exact certificate contraction: retaining a sound optimal witness yields maximal terminal WDL utility for that root. Under optional compute, additive terminal objective, no cross-root information coupling and resource feasibility independent except cost, a schedule can omit later computations of certified roots without worsening its objective; no assertion any extra search on unresolved roots improves real recommendation. Joint finite-work metalevel Bellman with signed utility and explicit certificate risk penalty; R* is minimax upper regret not bound for arbitrary action unless used as decision rule. Risk priority (2R*, unresolved contender count, fewer blocks, cyclic tie) is not expected VOC. Original Gibbs distribution retained in theory, not implemented this turn. 21918 initial exact assertions plus38160 correction checks (60078 total across the two distinct domains in corrected script). Primary Callaway et al arXiv:1711.06892 §4.2.1 and Sezener arXiv:1811.03035, Cazenave/Saffidine bounded search prior source. No generic scheduling novelty.
For four roots q=15 optional blocks each, K=60, S=sum(q-b_i)_+, E=sum(b_i-q)_+, U=K-sum b_i=S-E, C=certified-root subset of S. Correct attribution range [max(0,C-U),min(C,E)]. Initial CAL assertion C=S for all reuse arms failed before TEST; BOUND can move resources between unresolved roots as well. Preserved failure and exact correction C06B 7452927ffe33170b77cae65a19d42047e24f7844, before exact outcomes, no policy/data change.
