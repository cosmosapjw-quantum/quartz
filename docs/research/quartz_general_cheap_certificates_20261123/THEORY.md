## R05 — Constructive composite enclosure

Full derivation and operation-count proof are in theory/DERIVATION.md, obtained from native constructor /root/c24_theory and checked by the parent. For a linear sum of products with k roundings, expand every result into exact input products times factors(1+delta), plus at most k additive underflow errors. With |delta|<=u,|epsilon|<=sigma and ku<1, product deviations are bounded by gamma_k and error accumulation by F_k. This proves |computed-exact|<=gamma_k*sum|exact terms|+F_k; it is not empirical padding.

Ordinary stencil yhat has at most9 roundings, so Ey=gamma9*beta*X+F9. Ordinary subtraction then gives Er=Ey+u*(B+Y)+sigma for the fresh residual component. A sequential n-term dot has at most2n roundings: EP=gamma_(2n)*n*B*X+F_(2n), EQ=gamma_(2n)*n*X*Y+F_(2n)+n*X*Ey. The last term is the operator error propagated into x.Ax. Outward P=Phat±EP,Q=Qhat±EQ,ell=2P-Q enclose their exact stored-iterate values.

For D0=sum rhat_i² and rounded Dhat, positivity gives |Dhat-D0|<=g D0+F. Thus D0L=max(0,(Dhat-F)/(1+g)),D0U=(Dhat+F)/(1-g),where g,F are upward bounds at2n. True D differs by at most T=n*(2R*Er+Er²). Set DL=max(0,D0L-T),DU=D0U+T, using correct outward endpoints throughout.

Form raw qL=max(0,down(ellL+DL/beta)),qU=min(S,up(ellU+DU)); reject an empty interval. Clip in raw units BEFORE dividing by S, then divide outward to obtain utility endpoints. This prevents loose intermediate normalized bounds overflowing although true u is in[0,1]. Historical intersections and strict separation reuse C23's exact-optimal decision theorem.

Validation applies the same positive-dot inversion to sum b², yielding BL,BU. Require S>=BU and BL>0 for a nonzero RHS. Reject the known bmax2^-600 fixture because it lies below the declared2^-400 lower range. Certified relative residual is up_div(up_sqrt(DU),down_sqrt(BL)), avoiding a potentially overflowing DU/BL intermediate. PLAIN diagnostic is explicitly approximate and has no certified goal flag.
