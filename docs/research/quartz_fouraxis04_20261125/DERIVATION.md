# Certified budget reallocation
Let H=(H_1,...,H_n), residual work b, feasible root/local computation c=(i,z), deterministic work k(c)>0. For a fixed decision rule d_i, terminal utility U(H)=sum_i w_i E[u_i(d_i(H_i))|H], and risk penalty R(H)=sum_i w_i r_i(H_i), where r_i is the regret upper bound of the action actually chosen, or R_i*=min_a upper regret if explicitly choosing minimax-risk action. Do not substitute R_i* for bound on arbitrary NN/visit choice. For coherent beliefs and risk trade-off w_g,w_r>=0, V(H,b)=max{w_g U(H)-w_r R(H), max_{c:k<=b} E[V(H',b-k)|H,c]}. Cost-priced variant subtracts lambda C(c) (utility/us); hard stochastic time requires resource transition and possible overrun. Original prior-weighted Gibbs Q proportional Pi exp(beta action score), not implemented this turn; score risk is not expected gain.
Exact STOP contraction lemma. Suppose a root i has sound witness a*, retained irrevocably, terminal objective additive across roots, further observations of i cannot change other root information or resource feasibility except cost, and computation is optional. V_i optimal terminal utility already achieved pathwise; extra steps on i cannot increase utility or reduce certificate regret below0. Given any schedule spending work on i after certification, replace those computations with idle, retaining its witness and all other root actions. Under independent transition kernels/coupled random tapes of remaining roots this preserves other terminal output distribution and consumes no more resource. With nonnegative costs no worse objective. Thus an optimal policy exists that never computes on a certified root; theorem does not imply any heuristic allocation of saved work improves actual decisions. Cross-root knowledge transfer, mandatory output beyond WDL, shared hardware nonlinearities or state-dependent feasibility can invalidate the simple contraction argument. An actual MCTS recommendation may worsen after extra work, so RR is not pathwise improvement. Counterexample one unresolved root initially right, extra noisy search changes recommendation wrong. With coherent posterior reoptimization and ability to ignore extra observations, nonnegative expected information value holds; not for arbitrary approximate MCTS.
