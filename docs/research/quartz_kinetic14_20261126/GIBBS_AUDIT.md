# Adversarial audit of Gibbs usage
Static identity: for pi>0 and fixed scores u, beta>0, q*=pi exp(beta u)/Z maximizes E_q u-beta^-1 KL(q||pi). For any q, gap=beta^-1 KL(q||q*). This is not a derivation of the PUCT visit law, calibrated posterior on optimal arm, or an actual MCTS transition kernel. beta and score scale have gauge beta*u invariant (shift u by constant shifts Phi, not q; scaling u by k and beta/k unchanged). Fixed prior can suppress high-quality hidden actions: odds q_i/q_j=(pi_i/pi_j) exp(beta(u_i-u_j)); if scores in[-1,1], q_i/q_j<=exp(2beta) pi_i/pi_j. Positive smoothing artificially changes accessible support. A tiny prior cannot be rescued even by maximal score difference at small beta; beta->0 recovers pi, infinity concentrates score maximizers not necessarily true actions. With noisy scores, convex logsumexp gives E log Z>=log Z(E u); annealed log E Z >= E log Z, different objects, and cumulant expansion requires specified joint law/scale. Original paper p6's rho=0 as conservative upper variance is only justified under nonnegative covariance; rho=-1 gives (sigma1+sigma2)^2. Adaptive tree returns are neither independent nor identically distributed; W/M2 sample moments under serial exact backup do not make posterior credible intervals. Prior W1 shared bias floor survives infinite repeated draws; CHET/C29 history dependence. A, Phi, entropy, flip probability and MI are not terminal utility. METACOG13 actually worse-than-zero response prediction and unchanged final moves. Retain Gibbs as optional regularizer of calibrated computation utility or diagnostic, not as basic dynamical law. FEP generative-model free energy distinct from action KL. No physical temperature/Joule claim.
Finite complete search state H includes counts and topology, exact context ledger, evaluator identity, RNG and control memory; H->H' strictly increases N by block size. In this directed finite-horizon process a positive forward step has no reverse transition. On transient reachable N levels with positive mass, detailed balance cannot hold; a terminal absorbing measure is not evidence of thermal equilibrium. Naive forward/reversed support can be singular, not a finite entropy-production measurement. No Onsager symmetry or equilibrium fluctuation-dissipation theorem, no Jarzynski/Crooks without explicitly constructed reversible stochastic law and appropriate protocol. C26 detailed-balance/Lindblad toy construction is retained only as a different valid finite model. Instead introduce observable controlled occupation dynamics and forward path response.
