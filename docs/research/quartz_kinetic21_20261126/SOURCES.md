# Literature basis
Wainwright & Jordan (2008), Graphical Models, Exponential Families, and Variational Inference, §3 Proposition3.1, https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~jordan/sail/readings/wainwright-jordan-fnt.pdf : log partition derivatives are sufficient-statistic means/covariance; minimal representation caveat. Nielsen, Statistical exponential families: A digest with flash cards, Eqs7–8,15,29, https://arxiv.org/html/0911.4863v2 : KL with swapped natural parameters equals Bregman divergence. Derive directly below, no novelty claim.
Dawid (2007), The geometry of proper scoring rules, §§1,5.1, https://www.ism.ac.jp/editsec/aism/pdf/059_1_0077.pdf and SciSpace record https://scispace.com/papers/the-geometry-of-proper-scoring-rules-1kiwmp1vs9 : Bayes act induces decision geometry; log-score KL differs from terminal utility loss. Williamson & Cranko (2024), Information Processing Equalities and the Information–Risk Bridge, https://www.jmlr.org/papers/volume25/22-0988/22-0988.pdf : abstract-level information-risk connection, not used as theorem.
Russo & Van Roy (2014), Learning to Optimize via Information-Directed Sampling, Eq4–5 https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2014/file/90720a2fcc41f9332e6a1558da327089-Paper.pdf : optimal-action information and cumulative regret trade-off, not identical to terminal MCTS costed VOC.
Seifert (2012), Stochastic thermodynamics, fluctuation theorems and molecular machines, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23168354/ ; unified entropy production projection framework https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.2.033048 §§III,AppA : physical entropy production needs defined forward/backward process and environment; no such law for adaptive cumulative visits. Internal C26 Gibbs relaxation and K14 mixture identity retained with precise scopes. Hay et al and Russell/Wefald as K19, C29 finite Bellman. No priority claim.
