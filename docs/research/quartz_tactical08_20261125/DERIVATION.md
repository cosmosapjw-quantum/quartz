# Tactical coverage and resource-aware proof decisions
For unrestricted alternating Freestyle and Standard, let A complete legal set and F_p set of immediate legal winning moves. If F_p nonempty, T=F_p, every t in T has V*=+1. If F_p empty and F_-p={b}, T={b}. Every a!=b loses to the opponent's immediate win. Thus b is minimax optimal even if b itself loses, not proof of a win or draw. If F_p empty and |F_-p|>=2, every action is exact WDL loss: no unique forced defense; a fixed first legal certificate is WDL-optimal but may be poor versus imperfect opponents. GUARD STOP on immediate win or unique block, optionally sole legal action, not on forced-loss tie with multiple legal actions. CERT STOP on any robust witness. For a quiet position no new minimax claim. Preserve full proof intervals and prior.
For ranked candidate prefix r=min rank over T, serial fresh root, no noise, root PW k(n)=max(1,floor(2sqrt(n))) capped by legal count. Initial expansion at n=0 exposes rank1; selection before visit N uses n=N-1. For r>=2, first possible exposure requires N>=1+ceil(r^2/4). Floating f32 rounding at practical n<=4096 is checked separately. Under normal serial selection and no other widening, actual width after N visits is min(|A|,max(1,floor(2sqrt(N-1)))) for N>=1. Target exposure is exactly event E={r<=width}; visitation V subset E, native visit-argmax recommendation success S subset V for positive root visits (nonzero total and legal recommendation). In an output-only or zero-visit proof policy S need not imply V; do not pool with search arms or assign fake visits. For population of tactical target states, P(S)=P(E)P(V|E)P(S|V) with undefined conditional if zero denominator; failure probability partition 1-P(E)+P(E and not V)+P(V and not S). Counts from unique root families, not iid plies; past played action in pilot differs from native recommendation when temperature sampling active.
For r=45, N_min=508, r=25 N_min=158, r=140 N_min=4901. A fixed 256 visits cannot expose rank>31, because floor(2sqrt(255))=31. This gives an exact finite-budget obstruction to BASE coverage, not a lower bound for arbitrary search or proof. An exact tactic can be known with zero NN/visits; the proof scan has nonzero wall/CPU cost.
EXPOSE: target minimum candidate rank; root width floor r at every visit, same full prior order, same PUCT and descendants. Exposure is guaranteed after first visit but visitation/recommendation not guaranteed. Cost of materializing the intervening prefix O(r), not optimal arbitrary sparse expansion. FOCUS: same width and only first actual root visit forced to target (one worker, n=0), then identical PUCT. That guarantees target sampled, not later argmax or WDL on must-block. Exact terminal sample must return +1 in parent perspective, with W=N/M2=0 on that edge. For immediate win, missed target regret lies [0,1] unless a is exact -1/0, so use exact risk bound and known dominance, not pretend every miss is minimax loss. For must-block, non-target exact loss can equal target true value; tactical success not equal WDL improvement. GUARD and CERT do not compete on visit-coverage metrics. The comparison of EXPOSE vs BASE is whole policy change with cost, FOCUS vs EXPOSE isolates first forced sample under same floor; no counterfactual path replay claim.
