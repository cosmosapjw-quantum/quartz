# Definitions and scope
Orient board (a,b) to mover/opponent; unrestricted 7x7, 4-in-row, terminal win/loss/draw, alternating no pass. v in {-1,0,1}, normalized U=(v+1)/2. Exact per-action u(s,m)=-v(s after m), unless m immediately wins or fills board. R_h=max_m U(s,m)-U(s,a_h)>=0. Signed gain R20-Rh=Uh-U20, independently of action flips; unlike K39/K40 finite continuation labels this is evaluator-independent conditional on action. Common gate proves optimal action; exclude from hard exact corpus, include in actual arena. Late frame generated by safe nonwinning random alternating placements, not natural play or uniform legal-state distribution; selection only reachability, existing gate and D4 dedup, independent of solver labels or NN. Solver <=12 empties and 25000 unique attempted states per invocation; failed proof -> fallback L32 and preserve censoring.
