# R04

Three candidate policies under exactly one optional continuation per root: equal quantum/random fixed order, uncertainty (entropy), signed gain score. Derive g=p_change (p_plus_given_change E[D|D>0] - p_minus_given_change E[-D|D<0]); unchanged or equally-valued changed moves contribute zero. For nonadaptive allocation and equal marginal costs, top K conditional g maximizes E sum of D over K chosen roots by exchange argument (no independence needed for additive expectation conditional on all prefix data). With differing costs and hard deadline, deterministic problem is 0–1 knapsack if costs known; ratio is not generally optimum. A measured deadline scheduler can choose ordered chunk proposals without oracle; start only if remaining >= TRAIN upper-quartile cost, report overruns and unused capacity. Retain exact-prefix and posterior baseline no continuation as separate control. Do not confuse cost per completed visit with time.
