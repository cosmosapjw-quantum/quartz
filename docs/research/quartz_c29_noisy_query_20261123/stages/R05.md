# R05: new noisy-observation boundary and genuine contingent second computation

Construct independent binary U1,U2~Bernoulli(1/2), a useless binary latent Z with r1=r2=0, baseline incumbent b=0, and binary symmetric channel C(y|u) with accuracy 3/4. The primary STOP reward is max posterior mean. Before observing, T=1/2. Query candidate 1 once:
P(Y=1)=P(Y=0)=1/2. If Y=1 the posterior means are (3/4,1/2) and T=3/4; if Y=0 they are (1/4,1/2) and T=1/2.
Thus V_1=max(1/2, (1/2)(3/4+1/2)-c)=max(1/2, 5/8-c). STOP↔query boundary is c=1/8 (ties prefer STOP). At c=1/20, V1=23/40=0.575. After positive Y1=1, latent m remains 0 but STOP is 3/4 and a second BSC observation alone cannot raise expected maximal mean at positive c. This certifies scalar insufficiency in both terminal and continuation behavior.

A genuinely nonmyopic repeated-query fixture uses ONE unknown Bernoulli U~Bernoulli(1/2), known incumbent b=3/5, independent BSC accuracy 3/4 on each paid repeat, cost c=1/100 per query. Posterior after first positive is 3/4, after first negative 1/4. H1 value at start:
V1=1/2 max(3/5,3/4)+1/2 max(3/5,1/4)-c = 27/40 - 1/100 = 133/200.
Under the positive first observation, second positive has probability 5/8 and posterior 9/10; second negative has probability 3/8 and posterior 1/2. Therefore positive-branch continuation gives 5/8*(9/10)+3/8*(3/5)-1/100 = 311/400 >3/4, while the negative branch stops at 3/5. The H2 initial value:
V2= (1/2)(311/400)+(1/2)(3/5)-1/100 = 543/800 = 0.67875.
The nonmyopic gain over H1 is 11/800=0.01375, and over STOP is 63/800=0.07875. Query twice on positive evidence, once on negative evidence. The 2-step policy is genuinely observation contingent, not a repeated blind sample. All numbers are exact rationals.

Known-only mode with this noisy signal cannot choose an unverified candidate merely from its 3/4 posterior; hence a positive cost cannot justify purely noisy observations if they never become exact-known. This is a terminal admissibility difference, not a numerical disagreement. Perfect-reveal channel together with known-only terminal must restore the C28 behavior in the C29 oracle.

Scientific implication: information only helps when it crosses a terminal-deployment boundary or makes later computation profitable. The same number of observation bits may produce different value if utility payoffs or competing candidate posterior means differ. Any kernel speedup or relative entropy change is secondary to the realized resource-priced decision.
