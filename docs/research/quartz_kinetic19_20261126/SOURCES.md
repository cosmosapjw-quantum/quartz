# Primary-source and transfer notes
- Russell & Wefald, On Optimal Game-Tree Search using Rational Meta-Reasoning, IJCAI 1989, §3 single-step assumption, https://ijcai.org/proceedings/1989/0053.pdf : complete computation value = utility of revised decision minus default utility and cost; single-step approximation can fail through complementarities. This is the original intuition Quartz inherits.
- Hay, Russell, Tolpin, Shimony (2012), Selecting Computations: Theory and Applications, https://proceedings.mlr.press/r10/hay12a.html : Bayesian selection/metalevel framework, not a cumulative bandit objective; their finite sampling results require their model assumptions and do not certify our MCTS.
- Sezener & Dayan (2020), Static and Dynamic Values of Computation in MCTS, definition 4 and proposition 2, http://proceedings.mlr.press/v124/sezener20a/sezener20a.pdf : decrease in Bayesian simple regret and one-step optimality under corresponding knowledge states. Our conditional observation kernel is empirical and coarse; no transfer of their dynamic-optimal policy.
- Shperberg, Shimony, Felner (2017), Monte-Carlo Tree Search using Batch Value of Perfect Information, https://proceedings.mlr.press/r15/shperberg17a.html : batch perfect-information selection and intractability; VPI upper bound is distinct from actual finite noisy observation value.
- Callaway et al. (2018), Learning to select computations, https://proceedings.mlr.press/r16/callaway18a.html : value of information bounded by myopic and perfect-information values in their setting; metareasoning overhead matters. No claim their policy transfers.
- SciSpace discovery: Sezener and Dayan record https://scispace.com/papers/static-and-dynamic-values-of-computation-in-mcts-2al86676k8 ; 2026 Tuisov Search as Computation Allocation abstract https://scispace.com/papers/search-as-computation-allocation-el8554a3bwm4 , full theorem not verified, not used for proof.
- Internal K17/K18 derivation, exact native atlas and frozen labels; original manuscript and THESIS P1. No novelty priority claim for expected decision improvement, Blackwell-type coarsening inequality or empirical joint histogram.
