# Literature and scope
Allis, van den Herik, Huntjens, Go-Moku and Threat-Space Search, https://renju.se/rif/proof/Go-Moku.pdf §§3.1,4: a completion threat forces a reply, multiple completion cells can be decisive, restriction to threats may miss wins; Exa retrieved relevant passages. Use only immediate completion threats in unrestricted four/five-in-row, with explicit counterwin precedence, not the more general all-defenses heuristic. SciSpace https://scispace.com/papers/go-moku-and-threat-space-search-5c1evbeov1 and https://scispace.com/papers/go-moku-solved-by-new-search-techniques-499rbmit0l confirm original domains; no claim solved 7x7 variant. Parallel https://arxiv.org/html/2303.09449v4 §§III,IV-A, VIII: PN-MCTS true/false/unknown and distinction between disproving win and proving loss in games with draws. Our construction uses a sufficient win predicate for either player and sound WDL intervals; not PN-MCTS itself. K33/K34 common rule gate, K38/K40 visit-lock, K42 exact proof retained as independent evidence. No novelty claim for forcing search.
