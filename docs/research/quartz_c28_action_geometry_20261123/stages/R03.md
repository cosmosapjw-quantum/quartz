# R03 — definitions, assumptions and claim map

At a fixed observable state keep remaining candidates S, known incumbent b in [0,1], candidate probability laws p_i on {0,1/2,1}, signed loadings r_i with |r_i|<1, and query costs d_i>=0 fixed. Utilities and costs share utility units; r_i, f_i and latent mean m are dimensionless. A query exactly reveals U_i, removes i, updates b to max(b,U_i), and conditions the same joint law. Only known candidates may be deployed. A horizon of two is a total additional-query cap, not a receding-horizon claim.

Let mu_i=sum p_i(u)u and s_i=max_{p_i(u)>0}|u-mu_i|. For s_i>0 define f_i(u)=(u-mu_i)/s_i on support; use f_i=0 for a point mass and on zero-mass atoms. Thus E f_i=0 and |f_i|<=1. Z in {-1,+1} has weights (1+z m)/2. The conditional product law p_i^z(u)=p_i(u)[1+z r_i f_i(u)] defines a normalized nonnegative joint law. Initial m=0 preserves every base marginal exactly. A nonzero posterior m changes the remaining marginals coherently.

Loadings r_i are not pairwise Pearson correlation coefficients. Opposite signs represent antagonistic utility contrasts; no physical energy or thermodynamic temperature is thereby identified. The action-selection KL/Gibbs distribution in the original paper is distinct from this generative utility law.

Claims to distinguish: normalization/posterior/covariance are direct derivations; convex finite-horizon belief value is established POMDP mathematics; signed finite examples and explicit phase boundaries are project-specific derived results; speed is unproved until timing; actual model accuracy and better game utility from signed loadings are unresolved. C26/C27 game outcomes are already observed and will not become a fresh TEST through new seeds.

Numerical contracts will respect exact support failure, finite input validation, fixed tie priority (STOP before candidates), root-player sign and included controller overhead. Comparing posterior scores at different b or different remaining sets would not test the fixed-state action-boundary theorem. Original PDF independence-as-conservative claim is valid only with an appropriate covariance-sign restriction; for sigma1=sigma2=1 and correlation=-1/2, the contrast variance is3, versus2 under independence.
