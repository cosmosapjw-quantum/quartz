## Scientific findings
Given a fixed action utility U and before/after recommendations a,b, D=U(b)-U(a) is a signed decision improvement, not an information gain or flip probability. With equal compute quanta, selecting K roots by conditional expected D maximizes expected total D; if every prediction differs from its true conditional mean by at most epsilon, top-K regret is at most 2K epsilon. For conditional independence-free additive expected utility this comparison is valid even if different roots' gains are dependent; a score must still be calibrated to the actual intervention. The KL-regularized fixed-K Gibbs subset ensemble admits coefficient recursion over product(1+x exp(g_i/tau)); expected-budget Bernoulli relaxation yields a logistic rule. Distinct estimands: matched actual wall time, WDL, neural evaluations, search visits. None may substitute for another.
