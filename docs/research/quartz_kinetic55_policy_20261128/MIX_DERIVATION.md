At an extreme point, at most one cell has two positive probabilities and the rest are vertices, because four simplex equalities plus at most one active resource inequality allow at most five positive entries. Enumerate integer choices and mixtures along one increasing-cost edge; optimize exact fractions. Expected cost constraint is not a hard realized cost guarantee.
