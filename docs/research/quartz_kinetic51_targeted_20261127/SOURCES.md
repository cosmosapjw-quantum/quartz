# Sources and scope
Tolpin and Shimony, MCTS Based on Simple Regret, AAAI 2012, https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.5536 and https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/download/8126/7984: root recommendation optimizes simple regret rather than cumulative search reward; myopic VOI assumptions need care. Kaufmann and Koolen, Monte-Carlo Tree Search by Best Arm Identification, NeurIPS 2017, https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2017/hash/a6d259bfbfa2062843ef543e21d7ec8e-Abstract.html and https://scispace.com/papers/monte-carlo-tree-search-by-best-arm-identification-34k14qcwmc : abstract and section2.1 describe root best-arm identification and propagated confidence intervals; statistical confidence requires assumptions on leaf observations. Our deterministic neural child backups are adaptive and correlated, not iid leaves; exact WDL intervals are not statistical confidence intervals. No transfer of PAC/sample-complexity theorem. K50 matched games and K48 native proof/low snapshot are precise computational authority.
