# R05 constructive derivation
For a complete mask table f(m)=E[Y|s,w,m] with x_j=2m_j-1, expand f=sum_A beta_A product_{j in A}x_j, beta_A=2^-4 sum_m f(m) product x_j. Orthonormality follows summing any nonempty symmetric difference over its two signs. Uniform marginal effect of j =2 beta_j. Conditional effect at other mask x_-j equals 2 sum_{A contains j} beta_A product_{i in A minus j}x_i. Two-component difference-in-differences averaged over others =4 beta_{jk}. f(15)-f(0)=2 sum_{|A| odd} beta_A, so full-minus-base may obscure positive and negative marginals. For example f=.5+.05 x_S-.10 x_V+.12 x_S x_V, S average positive but conditional S effect negative at V off; full-base=-.10. Independent factor randomization is not a requirement for exact decomposition of a complete measured table; statistical generalization is separate. Compute coefficients for utility and NN and net separately; linearity beta_Y=beta_U-lambda beta_K. Worker contrast of coefficients diagnoses interaction with scheduler, not causal mediation by receipt.
