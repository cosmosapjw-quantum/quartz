# Native semantic preservation and budget recovery
K51 TARGET computes fresh child values u_i=-sum_{j=1}^5 x_ij/5, exact terminal point or clipped to WDL. At each fresh child, first expansion uses one physical call; each of five subsequent simulations uses at most one. Native restart preserves initial node, legal order, prior/value, PUCT argmax and tie break. Induction over simulations: equal node state and evaluator imply equal selected path, terminal status, expansion and negated backups; hence equal visits, edge totals, samples, action and calls in exact arithmetic. Floating tie-sensitive decisions require empirical parity; tolerance on numeric receipts alone is not a proof of identical argmax. No reuse of existing child in this equivalent port.
If instead reusing n past child visits with mean q and k new visits with mean z, new pooled mean q'= (n q+k z)/(n+k), q'-z=n(q-z)/(n+k). Difference bounded by 2n/(n+k), generally not zero, and z itself can change because PUCT uses accumulated visits. Example a old mean .9 n20, new mean -.2 k5, b old mean -.9 n20,new .3 k5: fresh favors b, pooled favors a. Thus reusing tree requires new experimental operator rather than optimizing K51 implementation under an equivalence claim. One can reuse immutable NN evaluations if cached exact, but count actual physical calls and preserve node initialization/trajectory; not implemented here.
