Invariant n<y where both exist. A successful witness is sound even if other branches were omitted; budget exhaustion never stored as a negative. Restricted negative is operational information only, not game-theoretic loss. Terminal/full-board facts unchanged. Depth extensions preserve exact root bounds, reset exhaustion status, preserve cumulative charged query count, and restart unresolved root loops; geometry and recursive cache persist. This is persistent bounded proof reuse, not suspended recursive-stack continuation. Early cert stops because remaining minimax regret exactly zero; stronger depth may give other optimal choices. Same action G=0 identically: do not subtract independent copies of its interval. If candidate optimal, intersect with G>=0; if baseline optimal, G<=0. If v_b=-1, any legal candidate is nonworsening; strictly positive only when l_a>-1. Unresolved replacement does not imply positive gain. Exact late action-value vectors from independent oracle refine bounds where available. In absence of full truth, width explicitly measures unresolved gain, not statistical confidence. If G lower bounds sum >=0, empirical mean true gain on that finite sample is nonnegative, but no transfer theorem, no posterior expectation, no guarantee of game-score dominance. Interval sums may be conservative and ignore shared constraints; same-action dependence handled exactly.
