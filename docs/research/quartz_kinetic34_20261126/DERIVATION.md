# K34 exact incidence scan, receipt identity and two resources
On unrestricted 15x15 freestyle5, a win exists iff a contiguous length-five window is all current stones. There are 572 such windows. Let W_p(s) be the union of empty cells of unblocked windows having four p stones, and P_p(s,a) be the union of the other empty cells of unblocked windows having three p stones and exactly two empties, one a. For legal a which is not an immediate win, W_p(s+a)=(W_p(s) minus {a}) union P_p(s,a). Proof: new all-but-one filled window either already had four p stones and a outside, or had three and a inside; all windows containing an opponent stone remain blocked. Conversely each term gives a valid remaining winning cell. Opponent W_q(s+a)=W_q(s) minus {a}, since no q stone added and only one empty occupied by p. Thus if opponent set minus a empty and |W_p(s+a)|>=2 distinct cells, win in at most three plies; no single reply can prevent both. Exact bounds [-1,-1] for immediate opponent reply, [1,1] for own immediate or dual threat, [0,0] full draw, otherwise [-1,1].
