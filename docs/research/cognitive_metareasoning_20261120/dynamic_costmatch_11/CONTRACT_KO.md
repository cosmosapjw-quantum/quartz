## Hypothesis, estimand, limitations

At a Connect4 root, Pascal Pons exact external oracle action score `S(s,a)`, illegal sentinel `-1000`, legal `u(s,a)=sgn S(s,a)∈{-1,0,1}`. Root regret `R(s,a)=max_{legal}u(s,a)-u(s,a)` is post-search only. For fixed two-step controller `D` and comparator `M`, primary comparison `Δ_R=mean(R_D-R_M)` with negative better. CPU cost = mean of **real actual Rust MCTS search wall milliseconds, resident root**; separate root setup, model load, full request and completed evaluator calls.

Neither a two-step commitment option nor a positive XOR example establishes a new optimality theorem or Bayesian preposterior KG. Conditional signed realized-gain Ridge models from C10 are frozen. The sole question here is whether actual **nonmyopic option** improves decisions relative to a **validation-only randomized cost-calibrated one-step policy**. Protect published negative findings and R1–R10 hypotheses.
