# Signed decision gain and robust selection
Fix prefix F, a0 measurable with respect to F, U_a in [0,1]. For each computation c with a fixed measurable readout d_c(Z_c), define G_c=U_{d_c(Z_c)}-U_{a0}, and g_c=E[G_c|F]. At STOP set G_0=0 and C_0=0. This estimand is the actual expected regret reduction for fixed full legal loss max U-U_d; not necessarily nonnegative. G belongs to {-1,-.5,0,.5,1} for exact normalized WDL. g = sum_{v>0} v P(G=v|F) - sum_{v<0} |v|P(G=v|F). This is also P(change) E[G|change] with zero gain when action unchanged, but change may yield negative or zero. K19 coherent ideal EVSI is a different quantity, and optimizes a readout under an approximate law. If all c share a correct law and baseline is Bayes, true Bayes channel value is nonnegative, but empirical signed gains for fixed readout may be negative.
Let v_c=g_c-lambda E[C_c|F], with price lambda utility per evaluator call, STOP zero. For estimates vhat, deterministic error e=max_c|vhat_c-v_c| yields v_c* - v_chat <=2e by adding/subtracting estimates and using argmax. In particular if vhat_chat>e then v_chat>0; unknown e is NOT a certified stopping/continuation margin. If individual estimates unbiased given F, E[max vhat|F]>=max v by convexity, and E[vhat_chat-v_chat|F]>=0 by pointwise max vhat>=vhat_c*, v_chat<=v_c*, taking expectations. Thus selection optimism can coexist with individually unbiased estimates, and K19's observed bias need not exclusively be selection. For a fixed readout fitted on disjoint A, gain fitting B conditional on A avoids using a training outcome twice to choose its readout and estimate that readout's gain. Exchangeability of B with future roots is additional, not guaranteed.
Exact trusted proof intervals l_a<=U_a<=u_a imply l_d-u_a0<=G_c<=u_d-l_a0 and global bound G_c<=max_{a!=a0}(u_a-l_a0)_+; if d=a0 exactly zero, and if full legal upper bounds<=l_a0 then no compute can improve action utility. This is an exact decision certificate under fixed utilities, not vote invariance or confidence. Immediate winning move gives [1,1], nonwinning action allowing immediate opponent win [0,0], all others [0,1] (full-board draw [.5,.5]); all proven losing legal moves allow STOP at any, and certified optimum can be selected before searching. Do not confuse proof with extrapolation from approximate NN.
