# L01 — Quartz primary-literature comparison

Date: 2026-09-07. Method: observational literature review, subordinate to the frozen `QUARTZ_RESEARCH_RECORD.md`. Scope: preserve the proposed small-network pattern recognizer, unified latent utility beliefs, value of computation (VOC), field/statistical-physics corrections, multimodal search and information geometry; identify mechanisms that could remain useful under repeated, expensive simulation. This memo establishes precedents and research questions; it does not establish Quartz performance or novelty.

## Main finding

The motivation is coherent as a research direction, but almost every individual ingredient has established precedents. Particularly, **joint correlated Gaussian value beliefs combined with VOC in MCTS already appear explicitly in Sezener and Dayan (2020)**. A defensible contribution would need a concrete mechanism and evidence beyond this combination: for example, a learned, structured latent covariance representation that transfers useful evidence between branches and successive searches, accounts for shared prediction error, and improves decision quality per total cost over a budget curve. This is a proposed contribution to test, not a finding that no prior method has done it.

The literature does not support relegating the motivation to cheap CPU search. Correlation-aware information sharing, prevention of duplicated evidence, better batch allocation and improved training targets can all matter when simulations are expensive. Whether Quartz realizes these benefits at high budgets remains an empirical and mathematical question.

## Serious conflicts to resolve

1. **Shared error is not fresh evidence.** Repeating a deterministic network evaluation gives no new observation of its prediction error. Independent simulator outcomes may reduce Monte Carlo error; retraining or new states may change epistemic information. A posterior update must state which of these occurred. The same-network correlation concern is explicitly discussed in [EMCTS, Section 3.3](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/bcbd670951a6dede2123961de19e5ea3-Paper-Conference.pdf).
2. **Adaptive rollouts violate an easy conjugate model.** The distribution of returns can change as the search policy improves. [Sezener–Dayan](https://arxiv.org/html/2002.04335v2) explicitly acknowledges treating a nonstationary process as i.i.d. in its practical method. Quartz cannot inherit Gaussian conjugacy and then silently claim its guarantees for changing rollout distributions.
3. **Decision uncertainty differs from marginal uncertainty.** For utilities with covariance matrix Sigma, `Var(U_a-U_b) = Sigma_aa + Sigma_bb - 2 Sigma_ab`. Common-mode uncertainty may cancel in action ranking. This is elementary algebra, not a novelty claim. An acquisition rewarding total latent entropy can spend heavily on uncertainty irrelevant to the action. [Correlated KG](https://people.orie.cornell.edu/pfrazier/pub/Correlated_main_paper.pdf) supplies a decision-centered alternative.
4. **Mode coverage and good decisions have different objectives.** Uniform energy-bin occupancy may overinvest in large but poor regions. A basin score needs the probability and utility consequence of changing the selected root action, not only novelty or histogram flatness. This is an inference about adapting [Wang–Landau](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0011174), not a failure claimed by its original paper.
5. **QFT language needs a mathematical contract.** Specify the random field, reference measure, objective, temperature and controlled approximation. A cumulant correction, Gaussian marginalization or log-determinant term may be mathematically useful; calling it a loop correction supplies no convergence or play-strength guarantee. No direct, validated QFT-to-AlphaZero advantage was established by this bounded review. This does not establish absence in the literature.
6. **Asymptotic convergence does not establish budget-curve dominance.** A constant bias floor, costly covariance maintenance or excess diversity can erase finite-budget gains. Conversely, high-budget gains need not require a better limiting solution: improved allocation constants and parallel throughput can matter. These are evaluation implications, not universal performance claims from any cited theorem.
7. **Search improvement and learning improvement are separate endpoints.** Changing policy targets can help training despite little immediate search improvement, and stronger search can reduce the number of training examples at fixed total compute. [KataGo](https://arxiv.org/html/1902.10565v5), [Gumbel](https://davidstarsilver.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/gumbel-alphazero.pdf), and [Reanalyse](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.06294) make both axes necessary.

## Concrete research opportunities, explicitly unproven

**A. Persistent structured beliefs over utility differences.** Use the small network to produce a compact basis for related positions/actions, and maintain an updateable posterior over latent coefficients plus local residual uncertainty. Derive policy and acquisition from that common utility model. Record observation provenance and representation versions so transpositions, repeated roots, pending simulations and retraining do not produce duplicated or stale evidence. Compare diagonal, full-covariance oracle, estimated low-rank and misspecified-covariance versions. The candidate differentiator is usable structure and persistence under cost constraints, not correlated Gaussian VOC itself.

**B. Joint batch VOC over diverse basins.** Treat the batch as one acquisition decision conditional on already scheduled simulations. A diversity heuristic can propose candidates, but choose batches for expected terminal decision improvement, measured against simpler pending-count and virtual-loss baselines. Test whether similar-looking branches give redundant information; a Euclidean distance bonus alone is insufficient evidence of information diversity. Coarse-to-fine search could allocate first between learned basins, then within them, but needs safeguards against merging rare winning branches with common losing ones.

**C. Controlled non-Gaussian corrections.** Begin with the Gaussian/linear case whose update and decision ranking can be checked exactly. Add a bounded mixture or a specified higher-cumulant approximation only where the posterior is multimodal or skewed and that difference changes root ranking. Compare with ordinary mixtures, Laplace approximations and Monte Carlo integration at equal cost. A field-inspired expansion earns its place by reducing a quantified approximation error or improving acquisition decisions; it should not be presented as physically compelled.

**D. Learn the marginal value of another expensive computation.** A small amortized metapolicy could estimate decision improvement per cost across simulation types, depths and basins, conditioned on remaining budget and posterior features. Train/evaluate on a budget distribution, including high-budget states. Compare against exact tractable VOC on toy cases, fixed network mixtures, Gumbel/sequential halving and the learned total-budget gate precedent. The hoped-for result is better use of expensive simulation, not simply fewer simulations.

**E. Repeated-search training loop.** Test three separately logged forms of reuse: within-root updates, retained beliefs across successor roots/transpositions, and fresh reanalysis of stored states after model changes. Evaluate whether saved compute translates into more useful searches or better policy/value targets. A reset-on-model-change control is essential because latent representations and prediction-error distributions can drift.

## Minimal evidence required for the stated high-budget ambition

These are proposed future comparisons, not experiments performed by L01:

- Freeze a competent PUCT baseline and at least one modern planning baseline such as Gumbel; use the same trained evaluator first. Separate later training comparisons.
- Sweep logarithmically spaced budgets extending beyond the original claimed regime. Report decision error/simple regret when ground truth is available; paired win rate or task return otherwise. Report uncertainty across independent seeds/problems.
- Match wall-clock and measured evaluator/simulator cost as well as raw simulation counts. Include posterior maintenance, proposal generation, batching and memory traffic.
- Cross low/high budgets with serial/batched execution and cold/repeated roots. The interaction between method and increasing budget matters more than a single pooled score.
- Include misspecified correlation, misleading small-network priors, rare decisive basins, deterministic duplicate evaluations and genuinely independent noisy simulations.
- Measure calibration on action gaps, covariance error, duplicate computation rate and realized decision improvement per cost. Use ablations to identify whether gains come from beliefs, allocation, mode proposals, reuse or training targets.
- Require no broad loss of performance at the high-budget end before describing the method as extending beyond a low-budget efficiency gain. A universal dominance claim would still be unjustified.

## Evidence and access limits

Confidence is high for the existence and the stated scope of the published precedents, particularly R02, R06, R07 and R09, whose relevant full-text sections were read. Confidence that the proposed combination is new is deliberately unassigned: this was a bounded comparison, not an exhaustive novelty search. The current preprints R12 and R15 establish contemporary overlap, not peer-reviewed validation.

Search families covered: rational metareasoning/VOC MCTS; correlated Bayesian value beliefs and KG; regularized/Gumbel planning; small/large policy-value networks; parallel MCTS pending simulations; learned/adaptive planning budgets; Reanalyse; Wang–Landau; information-geometric optimization. Initial broad discovery was followed by exact-title searches and primary publisher/author-page reads. Secondary aggregators were discovery signals only. SciSpace was available but not necessary once primary texts were located. OpenReview challenged direct access to the Gumbel forum; the paper was inspected at the author's exact PDF URL instead.

One access detail: the R12 abstract record reports v2 dated June 27, 2026, while the inspected v1 HTML contains an August 24 version note. Therefore this memo uses the stable title/authors/year and the explicit URLs, and does not claim a reconciled latest revision date. No substantive comparison depends on that discrepancy.

Stopping reason: all requested mechanism families have primary support or an explicit limitation; the most consequential overlaps and uncertainty assumptions were verified in full text. Additional broad discovery is unlikely to change the immediate decision to narrow the contribution and demand a matched-cost high-budget test. Root integration may prioritize fewer citations for readability.
