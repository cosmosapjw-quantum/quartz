# Finite directed response with controlled occupation
Assume one serial quiescent root, full immutable legal action order, positive completed visit count N, counts n_i and tie priority later rank wins. For a block of A forced and B free visits, vector f_i and g_i are actual counted root visits (sum f=A,g=B). m=n+f+g. Normalized occupation r'=m/(N+A+B) is exact mixture of prior occupation, forced injection f/A, and endogenous allocation g/B; for A=0 or B=0 omit that term. Conditional free occupation s=(n+g)/(N+B) is computable, not a counterfactual MCTS trajectory: free transitions depend on the forced evidence and modified tree. Let t=f/A. Then r'=(1-w)s+w t with w=A/(N+A+B); TV(r',s)=w TV(t,s)<=w. For any utilities U_i in [0,1], |E_r' U-E_s U|<=TV(r',s)<=w. This is an occupation effect bound, not a bound on argmax quality. For known partial panel use KINETIC14 sharp signed interval. KL mixing identity extends to three components by expanding logs: sum weights KL(r_k||pi)-KL(r'||pi)=sum weights KL(r_k||r')=I(component;action) in [0,H(weights)]. No physical entropy production or temperature; finite count process still irreversible.
Exact vote lock with forced future counts f and b unspecified free visits: baseline a after mandatory counts n+f is robust final recommendation iff for every j != a lexicographic (n_a+f_a, rank_a) > (n_j+f_j+b,rank_j). Necessity by giving all b to j (unrestricted possible count allocations, not necessarily realizable MCTS trajectory); sufficiency by all other completions. If true then extra free search cannot alter final recommendation under this contract. For current cap with no forced counts, reduces RAPFI11 exact finite vote certificate. Cannot conclude true minimax or improved play.
Forced readout diagnostic: a_raw=last argmax(n+f+g), a_sub=last argmax(n+g). Their difference indicates direct count injection matters at fixed observed trajectory; equality does not prove absence of intervention effects. Do not replace a_raw silently, and do not conflate raw-to-sub change with causal gain vs NORMAL. Counterexample n=(4,4), f=(0,16), g=(8,0): raw=(12,20) chooses arm1, sub=(12,4) arm0, any U0>U1 shows a reversal and loss, although raw occupation count has grown. Even infinitesimal w can change tied decision, so no argmax bound from TV alone. In this round raw final recommendation primary, sub diagnostic. Also measure actual intervention without forced votes (S/X/V/B), separating mechanical switch exposure and free-readout exposure.
For fixed root and finite schedule c, label U_k(a) by forcing game action a then both sides playing frozen Rapfi-evaluated standard MCTS policies to rule-terminal; k=PW128, FULL256. Fixed policy-conditioned utility not exact minimax nor independent iid observations. Let Ubar=(U1+U2)/2. Define signed Delta_c=Ubar(a_c)-Ubar(a_NORMAL), signs by strength retained; even equal label mean can hide opposing signs. Additional paired gain relative to STOP prefix measures total value of computation. Net Delta_c-lambda (C_c-C_normal), cost high-resolution process CPU ms, lambda=.002 utility/ms fixed. Policy comparisons at fixed visits do not imply matched actual time. Exact certificate and cost only are not an expected value estimator.
Observability: for independent source-family clusters, fixed finite menu, E_i=1{exists option with nonzero Delta}, H_i=1{exists positive Delta}, L_i=1{exists negative Delta}. A necessary data condition to fit signed gain is nonzero E_i; repeated options of same root are not independent. To avoid all-zero / mechanically created data, predeclared TRAIN gate >=6 exposed families overall AND >=3 positive families AND >=3 negative families AND >=3 exposed families among unforced options or forced sub-readout (relative to NORMAL). This is a minimum pilot design rule, not statistical sufficiency. If fail, do not fit. For independent identically distributed E_i Bernoulli(p), P(no exposure)=(1-p)^n; for 0 successes one-sided 95% bound 1-0.05^(1/n). Here sample selected by quiet-root and screening strata, not iid from natural play; report counts, no unqualified CI. Source stratification independent of test outcomes: screen via weak PW32 reference only; choose first of at most two quiet source roots with contrast between fixed panel actions. Outcome policies PW128/FULL256 fixed separately; correlated common model and board, not independent draws. Condition on admitted screen stratum; no inverse weighting for deterministic missing support. Preserve all attempts, no replacement. Unscreened still quiet and ply-selected, not whole population. Use exact independent role labels, no screen or test values in runtime.
