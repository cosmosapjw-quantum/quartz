# Geometric sequential decision value
Let fixed finite A, latent utilities U_a in [0,1], nested information F_t, m_t=E[U|F_t]. Positive fixed normalized base weights pi on A, tau>0 in utility units. F_tau(x)=tau log sum_a pi_a exp(x_a/tau), q_tau(x)_a=pi_a exp((x_a-F_tau(x))/tau). Variational F_tau=max_q[q.x-tau KL(q||pi)], gap exactly tau KL(q||q_tau). Use maximizing free utility (negative of customary free energy for energy=-x). Hessian H=(diag q-qq^T)/tau, categorical Fisher in utility coordinates g=H/tau; rank |A|-1, null span 1, quotient by common additive offset. This restores original Gibbs/susceptibility intuition, not physical temperature or MCTS sampling law.

## Exact finite displacement, drift and sequential telescope
For x'=x+v, substitute log q(x)/q(x')= (F(x')-F(x)-v_a)/tau. Hence
F(x')-F(x)=q(x).v+tau KL(q(x)||q(x')).
KL direction is pre to post, not posterior latent information. With coherent m_t and predictable channel c, E[v|F_t,c]=0; thus smooth EVSI G_tau(c)=E[F(m_{t+1})-F(m_t)|F_t,c]=tau E KL(q_t||q_{t+1})>=0. For arbitrary adapted score x_t, E change = q_t.E[delta x|F_t,c]+tau E KL; drift generally nonzero; positive curvature does not imply positive gain. Pathwise sum gives F(x_T)-F(x_0)=sum_t q_t.delta x + tau sum_t KL(q_t||q_{t+1}); for bounded stopping time and fixed pi,tau, calibrated martingale m gives expected difference = expected accumulated geometric term. If pi/tau vary, extra work terms, not this identity. Exact second-order integral tau KL= integral_0^1 (1-s) v^T H(x+s v)v ds. Local approximation .5 tr(H Cov(delta m)); remainder <= E range(delta m)^3/(6 tau^2), C26 reused. By Popoviciu Var_q(v)<=range(v)^2/4, exact upper bound G_tau<=E range(delta m)^2/(8 tau). Common-mode fluctuation gives zero, even if latent entropy decreases. This is a response susceptibility, not equilibrium fluctuation-dissipation or entropy production.
For hard max M(x), 0<=M-F_tau<=L=tau log(1/min pi). Thus |(E M(m')-M(m)) - G_tau|<=L (difference of two bounded smoothing gaps, not 2L). At tau->0, hard value recovered but local quadratic not uniform at ties. q and latent posterior are distinct. W1 covariance total law Cov(U)=E Cov(U|Z)+Cov(m'), latter is response covariance; repeated duplicate evidence cannot alter it if already conditioned. For a Gaussian observation y=h^T U+epsilon, response C=Sigma h h^T Sigma/(h^T Sigma h+r); common direction projected out by H. Same old W1 shared bias cannot be suppressed by counting duplicate evidence.
## Distribution-free decision-contrast bound
Let a* maximize m and delta_b=m_a*-m_b>=0. For b!=a*, X_b=(m'_b-m_b)-(m'_a*-m_a*) has zero mean under coherent observation. Exact hard value Delta=E[max(0,max_b (X_b-delta_b))]. This is expected signed improvement over a fixed initial Bayes choice, not vote flips. For any centered X with variance s², E[(X-delta)_+]=(E|X-delta|-delta)/2<= (sqrt(s²+delta²)-delta)/2 by Cauchy-Schwarz. Therefore
0<=Delta<=min(R, sum_b (sqrt(s_b²+delta_b²)-delta_b)/2)
where R=E max U-max m and s_b²=Var(X_b) = (e_b-e_a*)^T C (e_b-e_a*). Tight for two-point X-delta with constant magnitude, no Gaussian or iid assumption. At tie bound=s/2; Gaussian exact EI=s/sqrt(2pi) is lower for normal channel. At large gap use stable s²/[2(sqrt(s²+delta²)+delta)] (define zero for s=delta=0). This is a one-step upper bound only, not global STOP unless remaining complete information EVPI bound and all costs justify it. No confidence interval from observed Q moments.
