# C08: frozen 300-root engineering and retrospective utility campaign

A single frozen post-repair release binary (SHA256 c0fc82d0e98d943328c7a418065f9c277cdacb308dbc85822e964fc22a0f8a24) and the C07 TRAIN-fitted immutable 3x3 observation channel completed all 300 previously observed C26 game roots. Each root timed seven no-query baseline and seven noisy-controller native resident replays, alternating order. Raw requests/responses are preserved compressed in evidence/C08_GAME_BENCHMARK; total 2,100 paired timing samples, zero native process exit error. All 300 histories were checked by the independent C07 board/minimax/rollout-and-posterior verifier, including the queried seed, legal actions, observed entered nodes, separate offline exact result, and final posterior choice. The 60 CAL roots' action, outcome and node traces reproduced the separately frozen C07 validation. Independent reaggregation is in C08_RAW_AUDIT.json; no model refitting, measurement sign hunting or source change was done.

The all-root mean is NOT independent efficacy evidence: 180 roots supplied channel calibration, and all 300 were known from C26. The prespecified CAL mean realized delta is zero; actual rollout query price makes its modeled net negative, -0.001025390625. CAL total-run native controller mean time rises from 1970.60 ns to 9255.10 ns. Across all roots the ratio is 5.3320; this is substantially slower, not a full-game speedup. Query price (1/1024 utility per entered rollout node) does not include the extra *planner* CPU time, so the modeled metareasoning cost systematically underprices this implementation. There is NO matched CPU-budget strength gain or Elo claim.

Offline CAL channel outcome log loss conditioned on U_exact is 0.963502 nats/event vs 1.099717 for the TRAIN unconditional marginal (151 CAL candidate events). Thus this coarse channel has descriptive information about the exact utility, but that does not establish action-conditional calibration or net decision utility. Never equate a better likelihood score with a resource-priced policy improvement.

Status: bounded implementation and retrospective data provenance verified; scientific admission NEGATIVE on CAL resource-priced performance and whole-controller time. Future work should reduce/price the planner overhead and test action-conditional likelihood calibration on genuinely new roots, not tune the channel on this same CAL.
