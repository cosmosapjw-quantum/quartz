# R08: claim admission and scientific consequences

Accepted derived results:
(A) Under finite fixed utility worlds and specified observation channels, the normalized noisy-query Bellman and unnormalized homogeneous G recursion are exactly equivalent for H2 (indeed by induction for finite H).
(B) Under binary Z and conditional independence of fixed U_i and repeated Y_i given U_i, the factor q_z and candidate-local pi_i,z are an exact sufficient state.
(C) A scalar m alone is generally insufficient, as witnessed by an independent Z and a noisy repeated BSC observation changing local candidate posterior and terminal action value.
(D) A concrete H2 policy can benefit from a conditional second query: V2=543/800 vs V1=133/200 at exact rational fixed costs.

Numerically checked: 80 randomized exact models, 247 assertion groups, exact Fraction equality between factor recursion and independent complete-world recursion, plus Wolfram exact arithmetic on the decisive fixture.

Not admitted: new universal POMDP theorem, bound on total planning time, universal optimal threshold structure, fully calibrated noisy search channels for TicTacToe, new TEST efficacy, Elo or matched CPU playing-strength gain, physical/quantum interpretation. In particular, noisy rollout outcomes are not known to be conditionally independent given ONLY the exact minimax utility at each root; that is an *approximating observation model* to test rather than an established channel law.

The original Quartz resource-priced decision principle remains decisive: posterior uncertainty or information gain is not itself performance. A positive expected terminal value minus query cost can be compared to actual realized utility at a fixed policy and validated channel, but evaluation of that model and its CPU economics must be separate.
