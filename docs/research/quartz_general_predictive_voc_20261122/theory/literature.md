# R02. Literature and provenance

Hay et al. (2012) Selecting Computations: Theory and Applications https://www.auai.org/uai2012/papers/123.pdf; Callaway et al. (2018) Learning to select computations https://proceedings.mlr.press/r16/callaway18a.html; Sezener and Dayan (2020) Static and Dynamic Values of Computation in MCTS https://proceedings.mlr.press/v124/sezener20a.html support an existing Bayesian metareasoning framework, not priority or novelty for C20. Fleischhacker et al. (2023), A Closed-Form EVSI Expression for a Multinomial Data-Generating Process https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.00729 underscores the specificity of exact formulas to payoff/data models.
