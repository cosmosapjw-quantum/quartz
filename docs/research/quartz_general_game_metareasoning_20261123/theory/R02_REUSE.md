## Primary literature synthesis

Weitzman (1979), DOI 10.2307/1910412, gives the reservation rule for independent exact observations with fixed charges. The author’s May 1978 report Eq7 includes discount beta_i: c_i=beta_i E[(X_i-z_i)_+]-(1-beta_i)z_i. Setting beta_i=1 gives C26's expected-excess equation. Parent independently viewed the actual Eq7 page image, and retrieved MIT metadata and author-hosted published-paper search text. The full original proof is supplemented by a direct capped-value proof in C26.

Ortega–Braun's author manuscript Eq9–11 supplies the prior-weighted Gibbs variational identity; use those equations with the prior in the partition function. Lieder–Griffiths resource rationality supplies the expected-result-utility minus computational opportunity-cost objective, not a claim that every cognitive system solves an online Bellman equation. Friston's variational free energy requires a generative joint model p(o,theta); action-prior KL by itself is not that construction. Skilling's nested sampling has a prior-volume quadrature and shrinkage law; the legacy Poisson hidden-mode proxy does not implement it.
