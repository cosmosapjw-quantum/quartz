# R05

Constructive calibration bound: if S*=argmax_|S|=K sum g_i and Shat=argmax sum ghat_i, then sum_{S*}g - sum_{Shat}g <= sum_{S*\Shat}|g-ghat| + sum_{Shat\S*}|g-ghat| <= 2K epsilon when sup error <=epsilon. Proof: add/subtract fitted sums and use optimality of Shat; common terms cancel. No iid assumption, conditional g_i must include all selection data; adaptive/heterogeneous-cost extension not implied. Counterexample to flip-frequency/uncertainty ranking: A flips with prob1/2, conditional positive gain prob1/5 of 1, negative prob4/5 of 1/2 => expected -1/10; B flips with prob1/4 and gain1/2 always => +1/8. Allocate B. Also ratio counterexample with cap4 and (cost,gain)=(3,5),(2,3),(2,3): ratio chooses 5 while optimal 6; no universal efficiency from ratio. Thermodynamic extension: maximize sum_i p_i g_i - tau sum Bernoulli KL(p_i||pi_i) subject sum_i c_i p_i<=B; Lagrange multiplier lambda>=0 gives p_i=sigmoid(logit pi_i+(g_i-lambda c_i)/tau); strict monotone expected spend for c_i>0, tau>0. This is a soft expected-budget relaxation, not a hard deadline or physical temperature. For exactly K and equal costs, Gibbs law over subsets P(S)∝exp(sum g_i/tau)∏prior odds; partition coefficient of ∏(1+w_i z) at z^K, computable by O(nK) recursion. Low-temperature limit favors true signed-gain maximizers, not maximal flip frequency.
