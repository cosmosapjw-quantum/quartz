# Bounded literature overlap and construction
Nelson and Narens object/meta-level monitoring and control framework, via the explicit account in Fleur et al. Metacognition (2021) https://pure.hva.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/51745123/s41539-021-00089-5.pdf and Fleming, Dolan, Frith, Metacognition: computation, biology and function (2012) https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3318771/. Upward monitoring/downward control is established; not a new cognitive architecture. Shenhav, Botvinick, Cohen (2013) The Expected Value of Control https://www.cell.com/neuron/fulltext/S0896-6273(13)00607-7 control signal identity/intensity and anticipated payoff minus effort; a proposed normative account of dACC, not proof software implements dACC. Boldt/Gilbert (2022) partially overlapping neural correlates https://scispace.com/papers/partially-overlapping-neural-correlates-of-metacognitive-1lqlr26d ; no one-to-one module mapping. Ortega/Braun (2013) thermodynamics of decision-making https://royalsocietypublishing.org/rspa/article/469/2153/20120683/83817, KL information cost and free energy; Grau-Moya/Krueger/Braun (2017) https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7512193/ nonequilibrium changing utility, finite adaptation, overlap with work/utility transport (not claim new Gibbs identity). Sezener/Dayan UAI2020 https://proceedings.mlr.press/v124/sezener20a.html static/dynamic computation value and nonmyopic future computations under specific assumptions; do not apply their optimality to deterministic NN samples. Very close new preprint Shen/Dayan M^3 (May2026) https://osf.io/preprints/psyarxiv/ma2ts_v1 recursive POMDP meta-level perception/control, model-based/model-free adaptation; author abstract only checked, full theorem overlap unresolved. SciSpace index https://scispace.com/papers/m-3-meta-cognition-and-meta-control-with-markov-decision-98ui2b3k72gp corroborates. Vriens et al. 2024 meta-RL surprise/value/control https://scispace.com/papers/meta-reinforcement-learning-reconciles-surprise-value-and-k3437ssez3 ; abstract-level precedent. Exa/SciSpace/Parallel retrieved individual results after first parallel batch timed out; Wolfram actual algebra returned {0,alpha*(-m+r),0}. No exhaustive novelty search. Candidate contribution limited to exact joint observable decomposition on real resident MCTS and controlled response-memory/yoked intervention experiment, not generic metareasoning, Gibbs, brain analogy or new theorem priority.
