# R02: primary sources and exact transport boundaries

Primary sources consulted (URLs and version/section recorded):
1. Kaelbling, Littman, Cassandra, Planning and Acting in Partially Observable Stochastic Domains, Artificial Intelligence 101 (1998) 99–134, https://people.csail.mit.edu/lpk/papers/aij98-pomdp.pdf, §§4.1–4.4. Finite-horizon optimal value is piecewise linear/convex in the FULL belief distribution under finite states/actions/observations and fixed finite horizon. It does not prove that a one-dimensional latent mean is sufficient under repeated noisy observations.
2. Hay, Russell, Tolpin, Shimony, Selecting Computations: Theory and Applications, UAI 2012, https://proceedings.mlr.press/r10/hay12a.html. Bayesian selection/meta-level MDP and terminal expected-utility benefit minus computing cost. Its sampling bounds require special cases; do not transport universal finite sampling claims.
3. Russell & Wefald, On Optimal Game-Tree Search using Rational Meta-Reasoning, IJCAI 1989 (original), https://www.ijcai.org/Proceedings/89-1/Papers/053.pdf. Expected utility of a search step vs a one-step approximation; cannot equate single-step VOC with nonmyopic H2 optimality.
4. Monahan, Optimal Stopping in a Partially Observable Markov Process with Costly Information, Operations Research 28 (1980), https://doi.org/10.1287/opre.28.6.1319. Costly observation and optimal stopping predate this project; no novelty claim for the general Bellman construction.
5. Callaway et al., Learning to select computations, UAI 2018, https://proceedings.mlr.press/r16/callaway18a.html. Approximate metareasoning and value bounds motivate scalability, not a proof of C29 empirical efficacy.

Literature status: first two and Russell–Wefald abstracts/formulations located in original hosts; exact theorem text for Monahan not fully authenticated in this pass, so application is background-supported, not a checked theorem dependency. Source discovery included Exa, Parallel and SciSpace. No strong novelty or universal no-go admission.

Transport dictionary: hidden fixed utilities vector is POMDP hidden state; computation query is an observation-generating action; fixed per-query c_i is negative immediate reward; STOP followed by selecting the best posterior-mean candidate is a terminal reward; signed latent mixture is only one prior parametrization; horizon H2 is finite metalevel depth, not MCTS tree depth. C28 known-only STOP is a different terminal admissibility rule from the Bayesian selection paper, and cannot be silently substituted.

C29's derived contribution to seek: explicit candidate-by-latent sufficient-state filtering; unnormalized H2 recurrence with zero-support branches; a rigorous repeat-query counterexample to scalar sufficiency; and empirically auditable information costs. These are project-specific constructions, not a new general POMDP theorem.
