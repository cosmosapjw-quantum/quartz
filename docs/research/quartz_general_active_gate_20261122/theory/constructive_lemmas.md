# R05 | Proof and numerical conventions

For a Beta arm i*, if smallest possible posterior mean after its future observations exceeds competitors' largest possible future means, then argmax_j X_j=i* for every count combination. Terminal action does not change, so conditional true utility change Z=0 pointwise. Bayes gross EVSI is also0, even if observations have positive Shannon information. In sensor model statewise dominance similarly guarantees the chosen action remains posterior-optimal for all observations. Conditions require correct fixed utility set, exact bounded supports and source posterior, not a guessed neural MCTS probability.

For the upper bound, let D_j=X_j-X_i*. Because E[X_i*]=μ_i*, G=E[max(0,max_{j!=i*} D_j)]<=sum_j E[(D_j)_+]. From (D)_+=(|D|+D)/2 and Cauchy E|D|<=sqrt(E D²), obtain E[(D_j)_+] <= [sqrt((μ_j−μ_i*)²+Var X_j+Var X_i*)+(μ_j−μ_i*)]/2. The arm means are independent across arms after conditioning on initial evidence. From Beta-Binomial Var S=n a b(s+n)/[s²(s+1)], Var X=Var S/(s+n)²=nab/[s²(s+1)(s+n)]. This upper is finite, nonnegative, zero at n=0, and bounded by1−M0. Use explicit f64 tolerances to avoid overclaiming proof under rounding.

One-step optimality with known total expected costs would compare G to λ(C_task+C_meta). A run already paying C_meta faces sunk decision cost at continuation, so a correct ex ante policy must include the cost of invoking the gate. Here CPU costs use TRAIN median estimates, not guaranteed upper/lower bounds. Price-gate skips are heuristic and may sacrifice positive task gain; classify them separately from certified zero-information STOP. Actual measured post-compute actions are compared with an independently timed ALWAYS control, and no task draws occur under active STOP.
